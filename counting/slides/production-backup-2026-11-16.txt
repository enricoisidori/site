Production 

Date: To be defined Estimated total duration: 1 hour (2 hours maximum)

Requirements
5 performers + myself (6 people in total)
A space large enough to accommodate six people standing in a row (the studio is suitable)
1–2 cameras for filming

Performance
The five performers will not need to actively count. I will be the sixth performer and coordinate the counting, indicating when and how to change finger positions.

Filming
Fixed shot: A single static camera documenting the performance from beginning to end.
Multiple shots (optional): Additional camera angles, framing and creative direction by Contento360.
(In both cases, the video duration must correspond to the actual duration of the performance, with no time manipulation).
A metronome will be used during filming to ensure precise timing, with one beat per second (60 BPM).
Optionally, the metronome can be added back in post-production, with or without an additional soundtrack.

Outputs
Performance video: A continuous recording of the performance.
Edited performance video (optional): Multiple camera angles and optionally text overlays.
Explanatory materials by me (optional): Images illustrating finger positions and the number being counted.
